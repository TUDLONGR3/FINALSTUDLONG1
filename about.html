About NOVA

Our Story

Founded in 2014, NOVA began with a simple vision to create a modeling agency that values authenticity, elegance, and professionalism, we alsdo encourage people with abnoirmalities or dissabilties to join and feel seen, It is also a chance for people with disabilities Based in Baguio City, we have grown from a small creative studio into a premier agency representing talented individuals across the country.

We believe that true beauty is timeless. Our approach combines classic elegance with modern vision helping our models build sustainable careers while staying true to themselves.

Every face at NOVA tells a unique story. We are here to help you tell yours.

Meet Our Founder

ROSHAN PSALMUEL K. TUDLONG

His leadership has guided NOVA from a humble studio in Baguio City to a growing agency that celebrates diversity and authentic beauty in all its forms.
