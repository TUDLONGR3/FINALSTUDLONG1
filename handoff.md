NOVA Website Handoff
The brand
NOVA is based in Baguio City Philippines and the tagline is "Elegance in every frame"

the agency is big on inclusivity and welcomes people with disabilities
the founder and Creative Director is Roshan Psalmuel K. Tudlong
Services and pricing
Portrait Modelling starts at ₱2500 and takes 3 to 5 days
Commercial Photoshoot starts at ₱4500 and takes 5 to 7 days
Coaching starts at ₱6000 and takes 7 to 10 days
rush orders cost extra and you need to ask at least 3 days ahead

hours are Monday to Friday 9 AM to 6 PM and Saturday 10 AM to 4 PM

Strengths

Strong brand message the inclusivity angle makes NOVA stand out from other modeling agencies and it feels genuine and personal

Clear and consistent look all the pages share one style.css so the design feels connected from page to page

Easy to navigate the nav bar is simple and has the same five links everywhere

Clear services and pricing visitors can see what each package costs and how long it takes without having to ask

Good page flow the home page goes from hero to featured work to about to stats in a way thats easy to follow

Call to action buttons View Portfolio Learn More and Book Your Session guide people to the next step

Mobile ready setup the viewport tag is already in place so the layout can adapt to phones

Simple and cheap to run its static HTML on GitHub Pages so its free fast and easy to maintain and theres nothing to hack or break on a server

Personal touch the founder section puts a real face and story behind the agency

Good structure the files are organized in one flat folder so its easy for a new person to find things
